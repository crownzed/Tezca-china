# Phuong phap hoc tu vung tieng Trung dua tren nghien cuu

Ngay lap: 2026-06-08

## Nguyen ly cot loi

Khong co phuong phap "hoan hao" cho moi nguoi. Co mot phuong phap toi uu hon theo du lieu: bien moi tu thanh mot mang lien ket giua `hanzi`, `am`, `pinyin/tone`, `nghia`, `ngu canh`, va `hanh dong su dung`; sau do bat nguoi hoc goi nho lai o nhung khoang cach du dai.

Ten goi de trien khai: **RECAP-SRS**.

```text
R - Reveal: nap tu co huong dan, tai nhe
E - Encode: ma hoa hanzi + am + nghia + thanh phan chu + ngu canh
C - Challenge: goi nho chu dong, tang do kho tung bac
A - Apply: dat vao cau, hoi thoai, doc-nghe, san xuat
P - Personalize: phan loai loi va ca nhan hoa bai tiep theo
SRS - Space: hen lich on theo do kho va do quen
```

## Mo hinh tri nho cho mot tu tieng Trung

Mot tu chi duoc xem la "da hoc" khi co du 6 nut:

```text
Hanzi:        学习
Am thanh:     xue2 xi2, tone contour nghe duoc
Pinyin:       xuexi, tone mark/number
Nghia:        hoc, hoc tap
Ngu canh:     我在学习中文。
San xuat:     noi/go/viet duoc cau dung voi 学习
```

Mastery that su khong phai `chon dung nghia 2 lan`. Mastery nen la diem tong hop:

```text
mastery = 30% recognition
        + 20% listening
        + 20% context
        + 15% recall/production
        + 15% spaced stability
```

Gioi han mastery:

- Chua nghe dung: max 75.
- Chua dung trong cau: max 80.
- Chua on qua 2 ngay khac nhau: max 85.
- Chua qua 7 ngay van dung: khong goi la mastered.

## Luong hoc mot phien RECAP-SRS

### 1. Warm-up: 2 phut

Muc tieu: kich hoat tri nho va tao cam giac thanh cong.

- 5 cau on due de.
- Neu sai, dua vao hang `repair` sau 3-5 item.
- Khong dua tu moi ngay o 30 giay dau.

### 2. Reveal: 4 phut

Muc tieu: nap 6-8 tu moi voi cognitive load thap.

Man hinh moi tu:

```text
Hanzi lon -> nghe audio -> pinyin/tone -> nghia ngan -> cau vi du ngan -> collocation
```

Quy tac:

- Moi lan chi lam noi bat mot kenh.
- Beginner co nghia/giai thich ro; intermediate co the cho doan va gloss.
- Tu co chu phuc tap thi them radical/part, nhung ngan.

### 3. Encode: 4 phut

Muc tieu: gan tu vao nhieu moc nho.

Moi tu moi co it nhat 3 cue:

- `sound cue`: nghe va chon pinyin/tone.
- `meaning cue`: hanzi -> nghia.
- `context cue`: cau ngan co tu.

Voi tieng Trung, them cue `character-word`:

```text
学习 = 学 + 习
学生, 学校, 学习 -> 学 lien quan hoc
```

Khong bien phan tich chu thanh bai giang dai.

### 4. Challenge: 5 phut

Muc tieu: retrieval co do kho vua du.

Thang do kho:

```text
Level 1: nhan dien hanzi -> nghia
Level 2: nghe -> nghia
Level 3: nghia -> hanzi/pinyin trong 4 lua chon
Level 4: cloze trong cau
Level 5: go pinyin hoac noi lai cau
Level 6: tu tao cau ngan
```

Quy tac tang kho:

- Dung 2 lan lien tiep + confidence cao -> tang 1 bac.
- Sai -> giam 1 bac, feedback, gap lai sau 3-5 item.
- Sai cung error type 2 lan -> tao mini drill rieng.

### 5. Apply: 5 phut

Muc tieu: dua tu ra khoi flashcard.

Dung 3 dang:

- `micro-reading`: doan 1-3 cau co tu vua hoc.
- `micro-listening`: nghe cau/hoi thoai ngan, chon y.
- `micro-output`: go pinyin/noi mot cau/dien tu vao cau.

Cong thuc context:

```text
70% cau vua suc
20% cau co tu cu den han
10% cau kho hon de tao interleaving
```

### 6. Personalize: 2 phut

Muc tieu: app hieu loi cua nguoi hoc.

Moi cau luu:

```text
word_id, skill, correct, confidence, latency_ms, error_tag
```

Error tags:

- `meaning`: nham nghia.
- `tone`: nham thanh dieu.
- `sound`: nghe sai am.
- `hanzi`: nham mat chu.
- `context`: biet nghia nhung sai cau.
- `production`: nhan ra duoc nhung khong tao duoc.

He thong sau do chon bai theo loi, khong chi theo accuracy.

### 7. SRS: sau phien

Muc tieu: tri nho dai han.

MVP lich on:

```text
new correct good:     +1 day
new correct easy:     +3 days
wrong:                same session repair + next day
second good review:   +3 days
third good review:    +7 days
stable:               interval * ease, cap 30-45 days
```

Neu nguoi hoc sai sau interval dai, dung la "quen tot" theo nghia du lieu: do la signal de giam ease va quay lai context, khong phai that bai.

## Ty le noi dung theo cap nguoi hoc

### Beginner HSK 1

```text
40% guided reveal
25% recognition
20% listening
10% cloze cau ngan
5% production nhe
```

Chua nen day qua nhieu doc doan dai hoac chat tu do.

### Lower-intermediate HSK 2-3

```text
20% guided reveal
25% due SRS
25% context reading/listening
20% production
10% interleaving
```

Bat dau dung doan ngan va hoi thoai thuc te.

### HSK 4+

```text
10% reveal
25% SRS
30% reading/listening context
25% output
10% fluency speed round
```

Tu moi nen den tu bai doc/bai nghe, khong chi list HSK.

## Cong thuc phien 20 phut

```text
00:00-02:00  Warm-up due words
02:00-06:00  Reveal 6-8 new words
06:00-10:00  Encode: sound + meaning + character-word
10:00-15:00  Challenge: retrieval ladder
15:00-18:00  Apply: cloze/listening/output
18:00-20:00  Review errors + confidence + SRS schedule
```

## Cong thuc phien 5 phut

```text
00:00-01:00  3 due words
01:00-03:00  1 weak word repair loop
03:00-04:30  1 context/listening item
04:30-05:00  mark confidence, schedule
```

Khong hoc tu moi trong phien 5 phut tru khi backlog due = 0.

## Thuat toan chon bai de xuat

Moi ngay app tinh queue:

```text
priority = due_score
         + weak_score
         + error_specific_score
         + novelty_score
         - fatigue_penalty
         - recent_repeat_penalty
```

Mac dinh phien 20 phut:

```text
50% due SRS
25% weak/error repair
15% new words
10% stretch/interleaving
```

Neu due > 30: 80% due, 20% repair, khong them new.
Neu accuracy < 60% trong 2 phien gan nhat: giam new words 50%, tang guided reveal.
Neu confidence thap nhung correct cao: them retrieval de, khong tang level qua nhanh.
Neu correct cao va latency thap: tang context/output.

## Feedback dung tam ly

Sau cau sai, feedback nen gom:

```text
Ban chon: X
Dap an: Y
Vi sao: 1 cau ngan
Nghe lai: audio
Gap lai sau: 3 cau
```

Khong nen hien giai thich dai ngay luc sai. Sai lam tang tai cam xuc; feedback phai ngan, ro, co hanh dong tiep.

Sau 3-5 item, gap lai cung word bang cue de hon. Neu dung, quay ve bac kho cu sau 1-2 lan.

## Vai tro handwriting

Khong default chep tay moi tu. Dung handwriting khi:

- Nguoi hoc hay nham hanzi giong nhau.
- Tu co radical/cau truc quan trong.
- Muc tieu thi co yeu cau viet tay.
- Nguoi hoc tu chon che do luyen viet.

Default nen la:

```text
recognize hanzi -> type pinyin -> choose correct character -> produce sentence
```

Handwriting la booster cho orthography, khong phai trai tim cua app giao tiep.

## Dinh nghia "hoc xong mot tu"

Tu duoc gan nhan `learned` khi:

- Dung recognition >= 2 lan.
- Dung listening >= 1 lan.
- Dung context/cloze >= 1 lan.
- Confidence >= 3/4.
- Da on lai sau >= 1 ngay.

Tu duoc gan nhan `mastered` khi:

- Accuracy gan day >= 85%.
- Co it nhat 4 lan gap o >= 3 ngay khac nhau.
- Dung trong context.
- Neu co production mode: production khong sai nghia.
- Khong den han on trong 7 ngay.

## Ket luan trien khai

Neu ap vao app hien tai, uu tien:

1. Doi CTA chinh thanh `Hoc hom nay`.
2. Tao `LearningSession` gom item types: `intro`, `recognition`, `listening`, `cloze`, `production`, `repair`.
3. Dua SRS vao backend `UserProgress`.
4. Them `confidence`, `latency_ms`, `error_tag` vao submit.
5. Tinh mastery theo skill, khong chi tong dung/sai.

Day la phuong phap gan nhat voi "hoan hao hon" theo du lieu: it tai luc dau, retrieval dung thoi diem, context sau khi co nen, production co kiem soat, ca nhan hoa theo loi, va SRS giu tri nho dai han.
